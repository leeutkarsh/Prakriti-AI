Long-Term Protection

Prevent the disease next season.

Follow this seasonal roadmap to reduce the probability of recurring crop disease.

1

Before Planting

Choose resistant varieties

Select locally recommended disease-resistant varieties and use certified healthy seeds or seedlings.

2

Planting

Improve spacing & airflow

Avoid overcrowding. Good plant spacing helps leaves dry faster and reduces prolonged moisture.

3

Growing Season

Monitor leaves weekly

Inspect leaves regularly and upload suspicious images as soon as symptoms appear.

4

Irrigation

Keep foliage dry

Prefer irrigation methods that minimize unnecessary leaf wetness.

5

After Harvest

Remove infected plant material

Sanitize tools and remove diseased plant debris according to local agricultural recommendations.
